Behind the Camera

We spend most weddings not saying "look here."

Our approach is quiet by design — we photograph the day as it actually unfolds: the pause before vows, the joke that gets the whole table laughing, the walk back to the car at midnight. Little direction, lots of attention.

Arneja Studio started as a photojournalism practice before turning fully to weddings and portraits in 2018. That background still shapes how we shoot — we're looking for the true moment, not the arranged one.
